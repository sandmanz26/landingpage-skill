How does the process work?

It starts with a discovery call, where we learn about your organization, your research workflows, the challenges you want to solve and who's involved in the decision. We then schedule a tailored walkthrough of Bipsync built around what we heard.

Who should join?

For the discovery call, whoever is leading the search is perfect. For the walkthrough, bring the people involved in the decision: investment, operations, compliance and IT colleagues are all welcome.

Will you cover security and moving from our current system?

Yes. Bipsync is SOC 2 and ISO 27001 Certified, and our team manages migration from legacy systems, shared drives and notes. See Security & Governance and Deployment & Support.
