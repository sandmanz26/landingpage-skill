Playfolly is what happens when architecture meets a treehouse. Modular, customisable, and built to last. Designed for beautiful play.

A treehouse, without a tree.

Lifted off the ground to create the feeling of climbing high, with a tall, slender form that fits neatly into real gardens.

Dangerously safe.

An inherently stable conical form and broad stance resist toppling by design, with ground anchors for added assurance — designed to ISO 8124:4, with New Zealand certification pending.

Match your mood. Or your moodboard.

Choose almost any colour to complement your home or express your child’s personality — then configure your layout and swap accessories as you go.

Weather the weather, whatever the weather.

Made from powder-coated aluminium and marine-grade ply, wrapped in a macrocarpa rainscreen, Playfolly is built for life outdoors. Tested in the coastal winds of Wellington, New Zealand. Rain, sun, salt air — built to last

Grows with your family.

Designed for ages three and up — a place to climb and imagine, and later to sit, read, and escape. From little adventures to bigger ones.

Good for your kids. Good for the planet.

Made with sustainably sourced timber and some of the world’s lowest-carbon aluminium — no plastic, no screens, just real-world play

Mow with the flow.

Aluminium legs provide clearance for mowing and trimming, allowing sunlight and rain to reach the grass below. Your lawn never looked better.

Play today. Pass it on tomorrow. 

Designed for over a decade outdoors, with a modular system that can evolve, be repurposed, or be passed on.

At over 2.4m high, Playfolly is tall and slender, with a compact footprint and elevated platform. Powder-coated aluminium, marine-grade ply, and macrocarpa timber form a durable structure built for life outdoors. Supplied with ground anchors for secure installation.

Delivered flat-packed as a modular system, it can be assembled by two adults in a morning.
