FAQS
WHO CAN TAKE PRIVATE LESSONS?

Private lessons are open to kids and adults of all ages and skill levels, from complete beginners to advanced players. Lessons are available for individuals or groups of up to 6 students.

ARE STUDENTS EXPECTED TO BRING RACQUETS & WATER?

Students are expected to bring their own racquet and water to the tennis lesson and come dressed in athletic clothing with athletic shoes.

If you are in need of equipment, we recommend the tennis brand Yonex, available at Tad’s Sporting Goods – located at 70th and Granville. The staff at Tad’s are knowledgeable and will be able to provide you with the right recommendations for racquets and any other gear that you might need. They also have the best selection of used tennis racquets in the city.  All students of Summer Smash Tennis receive a discount at Tad’s Sporting Goods.

DO I PAY ON THIS WEBSITE OR ACTIVENET?

Private tennis lessons are offered in partnership with the City of Vancouver.  Booking private lessons requires you to complete payment through the City of Vancouver’s ActiveNet website before you confirm your booking time here through Summer Smash Tennis.

All payments are processed through the City of Vancouver ActiveNet portal. Click the price in the table above to go directly to the correct link. After paying, return here to book your lesson time and select number of attendees. Please note that it will show $0 on the booking checkout since payment is completed separately on ActiveNet.

You will need to create an ActiveNet account if you do not already have one to complete payment.

WHAT RECEIPT DO I NEED TO UPLOAD?

Please upload the receipt you receive after completing payment on the City of Vancouver ActiveNet website. Save this receipt as a PDF or screenshot after purchasing, make sure the receipt number and total lessons purchased is clearly visible – you’ll need to upload it each time you book an individual lesson.

HOW DO I ACCESS MY PAYMENT RECEIPTS ON ACTIVENET?

You can access receipts online via your ActiveNet account.

To View Past Receipts and Payment History:

Login to ActiveNet – Click “My Account ” (top right corner) 
Select “Get a List of Prior Payments” or “Transaction and Payment History” to print/view receipts.
