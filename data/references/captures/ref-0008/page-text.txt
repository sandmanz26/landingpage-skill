FAQs
Why is your whole-person approach more effective than standard health programs?
Most health programs try to force-fit you into predefined boxes - “you’re overweight” or “you have low energy.” We recognize that your fatigue might be connected to your gut health, which affects your sleep, which impacts your stress levels, which influences your food choices. By mapping these connections through advanced diagnostics and AI, we can identify the root patterns unique to you. Sometimes the most effective solution isn’t the most obvious one - fixing your sleep might do more for your weight goals than another diet plan.
How do Precision Wellness Stacks differ from typical supplement routines?
Think of Precision Wellness Stacks as your life optimization toolkit. Unlike rigid supplement regimens, these stacks work in the moment, when you need them most - whether you’re seeking mental clarity before a big meeting, recovery after celebrations, better sleep despite jet lag, or an optimized power nap. Each stack combines proven elements that work together: targeted nutrients, behavioral protocols, and physiological techniques. This isn’t about taking pills - it’s about giving your body exactly what it needs, precisely when it needs it, to perform at its best
What health goals can I pursue?
Whether you’re focusing on cognition, sleep, focus, memory or beauty, we recognize these goals are interconnected. Resolute offers you 10 unique goals to choose from. Working on fat loss? We’ll consider your sleep quality and stress levels. Targeting memory? We’ll look at your gut health and inflammation markers. This interconnected approach means you make progress across multiple areas, even while focusing on specific goals.
Why 100 days?
Real transformation isn’t about quick fixes or 30-day challenges. Our 100-day framework is designed around the neuroscience of habit formation and cellular renewal cycles. It gives your body time to adapt, your habits time to solidify, and your biomarkers time to shift meaningfully. This timeline lets us work with your body’s natural rhythms rather than forcing artificial deadlines.
How do you balance wellness optimization with real life?
Life is meant to be lived, not spent obsessing over health metrics. We help you find your personal sweet spot between optimization and enjoyment. Want to enjoy social drinks sometimes? We’ll help you do it smarter. Love late-night work sessions? We’ll optimize your recovery. The goal isn’t perfection - it’s sustainable excellence.
How involved are your experts?
Our experts don’t just check boxes or follow scripts. They collaborate to understand your unique patterns and challenges. Your data informs their recommendations, but their experience helps interpret what the numbers mean for your daily life. They’re your partners in optimization, not distant authorities.
What does progress really look like?
Progress isn’t just about numbers on a scale or test results (though we measure those too). It’s about waking up with more energy, handling stress better, thinking more clearly, and feeling more resilient. Our members often notice subtle improvements first - better sleep quality, more stable mood, fewer energy crashes - before seeing bigger shifts in their biomarkers and body composition.
What makes your diagnostic testing different?
Our comprehensive diagnostics aren’t just about collecting data - they’re about understanding your body’s story. From epigenetic age testing and full-body MRIs to gender-specific blood profiles and comprehensive gut screenings, each test you select maps connections between different aspects of your health. This helps us create precise interventions that work with your body’s natural patterns, not against them.
