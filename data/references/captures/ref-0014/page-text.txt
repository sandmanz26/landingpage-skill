Comfort at your fingertips that only gets smarter

Quilt is a single system made of four parts. The Quilt Indoor Unit, Outdoor Unit, Dial, and App work together to deliver the world’s smartest home climate solution.

At only 7 ⅞ in tall, Quilt Indoor Units fit seamlessly into more spaces while saving energy through occupancy detection and continuous improvements via over-the-air updates.

HOW IT WORKS
Control every room in your home from any room in your home

Ductless systems put you in control — set each room to its perfect temperature so everyone’s comfortable. Quilt is even more efficient with Auto-Away, which uses intelligent occupancy sensing to automatically save energy when rooms are empty.
