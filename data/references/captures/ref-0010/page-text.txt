WHAT IS A-RETE HIGH PERFORMANCE TENNIS ACADEMY?

A-Rete Academy is an internationally-staffed, high level coaching and training center designed to develop and foster the professional growth of elite tennis players of all ages, with long-stay, intensive training programs designed to guide you along the pathway that leads to higher and higher tournament circuits until you’re ready to compete at the top world level. We’ll be glad to evaluate your application to any of these open spots, but these are our criteria for acceptance into the Academy

Discipline and Commitment

YOU MUST PROVE SINCERE FULL-TIME DEDICATION TO TENNIS TRAINING AND COMPETITION.
THIS MEANS BEHAVING AS A PROFESSIONAL TRAINING PLAYER, MAINTAINING CONSISTENCY IN ATTENDING SESSIONS, AND KEEPING YOUR TRAINING LOGS.
PRIORITY IN SELECTION WILL ALWAYS BE GIVEN TO PLAYERS ENROLLING IN FULL-TIME, YEAR-ROUND TRAINING PROGRAMS.

Competitive Drive

YOU MUST SHOW A STRONG PERSONAL DRIVE TO IMPROVE PHYSICALLY, TECHNICALLY, AND MENTALLY, AND A PASSION FOR COMPETING AT HIGH-LEVEL EVENTS.

Performance history and ranking

ALONGSIDE YOUR PERSONAL DEVELOPMENT TRAJECTORY AND SENSIBLE EXPECTATIONS FOR YOUR AGE GROUP, HAVING A CURRENT ITF/TE/ATP/WTA RANKING OR EQUIVALENT, WITH A STORY OF COMPETITIVE SUCCESS ON THE NATIONAL AND INTERNATIONAL LEVEL, WILL HOLD SIGNIFICANT IMPORTANCE. PREFERENCE WILL BE GIVEN TO PROFILES INCLUDING COMPETITION IN

National Championship Circuits
Tennis Europe (TE)
ITF Junior/Pro Circuit
ATP/WTA-sanctioned events
Developmental Stage & Growth Potential

YOU WILL BE EVALUATED BY A REVIEW OF YOUR BIOLOGICAL AND CHRONOLOGICAL AGE AND HOW IT IS IN LINE WITH CURRENT PERFORMANCE DATA. THE CEILING FOR YOUR FUTURE GROWTH, BASED ON ATHLETICISM, TECHNICAL BASE, AND MATURITY, WILL BE TAKEN INTO ACCOUNT – AND WE ACTIVELY ENCOURAGE YOUNGER PLAYERS WITH HIGH-POTENTIAL PROFILES TO APPLY.

Coachability

YOU WILL HAVE TO PROVE ABILITY TO RESPOND POSITIVELY TO STRONG, STRUCTURED COACHING, ABSORBING FEEDBACK AND ADJUSTING YOUR TACTICS AND TECHNIQUE ACCORDINGLY, WITH AN OPEN ATTITUDE TO LEARNING UNDER STRONG PRESSURE. THIS CRITERION IS PARAMOUNT TO US, SO MUCH SO THAT AT THE DISCRETION OF THE DIRECTOR OF HIGH PERFORMANCE THESE QUALITIES MIGHT EARN YOU A SPOT FOR CONSIDERATION EVEN IF YOU DO NOT HAVE A CURRENT RANKING.

Trial Period and Assessment

YOU WILL UNDERGO A MANDATORY TRIAL AND TESTING PERIOD FOR A TIME DEPENDING ON YOUR LEVEL, AND IN ANY CASE NO LESS THAN A MONTH. THIS WILL INCLUDE ON-COURT AND BEHAVIORAL ASSESSMENTS, BOTH IN INDIVIDUAL AND GROUP SETTINGS, AND PHYSICAL TESTING, AND ALL RESULTS WILL BE REVIEWED BY THE HIGH-PERFORMANCE TEAM FOR FINAL SELECTION.

WHERE YOU’LL BE LIVING, TRAINING, AND LEARNING

Accomodation

APARTMENTS ARE AVAILABLE FOR YOU DIRECTLY ON-SITE, IN A BEAUTIFUL ESTATE SET IN A QUIET, RELAXING LOCATION THREE MILES FROM A REGIONAL PINEWOOD PARK, IN THE SLIGHTLY HILLY COUNTRYSIDE OF LOMBARDY. Logistics
