# Copy in Bahasa Indonesia

- **Choose the address once, from the brand:** "Anda" (formal, professional services, health,
  finance) or "kamu" (casual, youth, lifestyle). Never mix them on one page. Write the choice in
  the spec.
- **Write in Indonesian, don't translate.** Avoid literal calques of English marketing ("tingkatkan
  bisnis Anda ke level berikutnya", "solusi all-in-one"). Say the concrete thing: "Tagihan terkirim
  lewat WhatsApp, dibayar rata-rata 3 hari lebih cepat".
- **Length.** Indonesian usually runs longer than the English equivalent. Fit headlines to two lines
  using the real Indonesian text, and test the longest one in the skeleton.
- **Mixed language** is fine when the audience speaks that way (common product terms: "checkout",
  "dashboard"), but pick it deliberately and keep it consistent.
- **Numbers and money:** "Rp 189.000" (dot for thousands, no decimals), "24 jam", dates as
  "12 Januari 2026". Phone numbers in a format people can tap: +62 812-3456-7890.
- **Buttons say what happens:** "Pesan lewat WhatsApp", "Lihat jadwal dokter", not "Klik di sini" or
  "Submit".
- **Banned stock phrases** are in `rules/lint-words.json`; the team can add more.
