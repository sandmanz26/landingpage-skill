# Critique: Jalinan Wedding Organizer (with-skill)

Independent critic, evidence pack only (digest.md, desktop-fold, mobile-fold, sheet-desktop,
sheet-mobile, desktop-page-02, desktop-page-05). No code opened. Business facts are samples and were
not judged.

Not verified: FAQ answers (all seven items are collapsed in every screenshot and the digest's page
text has no answer text, so I cannot tell whether the answers are good, or whether they exist).
Mobile pages were judged from the contact sheet only, at small size. Motion was not judged beyond
the digest numbers.

## Passes (short)
1. One second: the headline "Hari H berjalan. Kalian tinggal hadir." on dark green, with the paper
   rundown card as the second mass. One answer, not "a website".
2. Five seconds: eyebrow "WEDDING ORGANIZER · JAKARTA", headline, yellow WhatsApp button, price
   from Rp 9,5 juta. What, where, and next step are all on the first screen. Passes.
3. Squint: desktop fold reduces to headline block + paper card + yellow button. Good. Lower page
   alternates cream / dark green / navy bands, each with one heading mass; fine.
4. Cover the logo: the rundown card with crew names per line, "fee" next to every price, the
   30/40/30 stage line and "Kalian → vendor / Kalian → Jalinan" could not be used by an all-in promo
   WO. But Tanya jawab (accordion) and the closing band (navy, 1-2-3 steps) are stock blocks. And
   nothing on the page says "wedding" except the words: no motif, no image, no warmth. Palette and
   condensed type read as a logistics or legal service.
5. Edges: one main left edge (about x=232 desktop) held through every section; the second column
   edges (cards, Hari H list) are consistent. Composed.
6. Space: section gaps clearly larger than inner gaps. One hole: desktop-page-05, the left column of
   Hari H ends after two short paragraphs and leaves about 270 px of empty dark green beside the
   four "Kalau ..." pairs.
7. Type: decisive jump body to headline (74 px Archivo condensed vs 16-17 px body). But the
   inspector counts 15 font sizes (WARN too-many-font-sizes). Mono is used for labels, times and
   the hero price note; the note "Koordinasi hari H mulai Rp 9,5 juta..." in mono at body width is
   harder to read than the body above it.
8. Colour: dark green and cream hold the surface, navy supports, yellow is the accent. Yellow is
   used for all CTAs and also for highlights (Akad nikah, Resepsi dibuka, "Biaya tambahan: tidak
   ada", the Hari H table row, the "Kalau ..." labels). About ten places: the accent is diluted.
9. Pictures: none (brief: no imagery exists). The rundown card is a drawn object that does a job.
   No grey boxes, no fake photos. Possible stray white dot at the top-left corner of the
   Pendampingan card (desktop-page-02, about x=570 y=152); may be an intended punch hole, unclear.
10. Sameness: hero = document, harga = ticket cards, cek kami = checklist, persiapan = table,
    hari H = split list, tanya jawab = accordion, CTA = steps. No repeat in a row.
11. Phone: its own stack, sticky WhatsApp bar. But the first screen is crowded (see problem 1).
12. Completeness: price, money flow, legal checks, timeline, day-of plan, address, hours,
    WhatsApp number all present. FAQ answers not visible.
13. Fit: no team anchors given. Against the brief (no invented proof, trust after fraud news) it
    fits: proof is all checkable policy, no testimonials.

## Top three problems
1. Phone first screen is crowded and partly broken — mobile-fold.png, top bar and lower third —
   the logo subline and the header "Cek tanggal" button each wrap to two lines, three yellow
   buttons compete on one screen (header, hero, sticky bar), and the rundown card, the page's idea,
   is cut off and partly covered by the sticky bar. The first screen on the device most couples use
   loses its one clear thing — smallest fix: on phones, drop the header button (the sticky bar
   already does that job) and the "WEDDING ORGANIZER" subline (the eyebrow repeats it), so the
   card rises; or show the sticky bar only after the hero button scrolls out.
2. The biggest fraud fear is hidden — sheet-mobile mobile-page-09 / sheet-desktop desktop-page-06,
   Tanya jawab — "Bagaimana kalau kami batal?" and "Kenapa pelunasan setelah acara?" are collapsed
   behind "+" icons, and no answer text is visible anywhere in the evidence. For a reader who came
   because of WO fraud news, "what happens to my DP if we cancel" is a deciding question, not a
   leftover — smallest fix: move a one-line cancellation rule into the Harga stage line (under the
   first 30%), and render the FAQ answers open (or at least these two).
3. Desktop loses the action for half the page — desktop-page-04 to desktop-page-06 (Persiapan,
   Hari H, Tanya jawab) — no button or link is visible between the mid CTA (31%) and the closing
   band (89%), and the header does not stay. The Hari H "Kalau ..." pairs are the most convincing
   part and the reader has nowhere to act on them — smallest fix: make the header sticky on desktop
   (it already holds "Cek tanggal"), or add one text-link CTA under the Hari H pairs, which also
   fills the empty left column there (desktop-page-05).

## Keep
- The rundown card in the hero (desktop-fold): times, crew first names per line, "CONTOH" stamp.
  It is the WO's real working document, proves competence without invented proof, and gives the
  fold its second mass.
- The money block in Harga (desktop-page-02): "fee" beside every price, the 30/40/30 stage line
  tied to milestones, "Biaya tambahan: tidak ada" with what is included, and the two
  "Kalian → vendor / Kalian → Jalinan" boxes. This is the position made readable in five seconds.
- The "Kalau ..." failure → response pairs in Hari H (desktop-page-05) and the "Takut ketemu WO
  bodong? Wajar. Cek kami dulu." checklist: concrete, in the customer's words, checkable.

## Fixes applied (round 1)
1. Phone first screen: header CTA and logo subline hidden on phones; the sticky WhatsApp bar slides in only after the hero button has scrolled away.
2. Cancellation rule in one line under the payment stages; "Kenapa pelunasan setelah acara?" and "Bagaimana kalau kami batal?" open by default.
3. Header sticky on all widths; a CTA in the empty left column of the Hari H section.
Also: the stray white dot on the featured price slip removed.
