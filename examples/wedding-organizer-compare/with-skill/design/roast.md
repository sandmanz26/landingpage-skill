## Pre-mortem
1. The client's real model differs (holds vendor money, sells packages, takes a large DP); the roof is unusable, or worse, the page ships with sample claims and the client is accused of the overpromising it was meant to avoid.
2. Couples arrive from Instagram, find documents and no weddings, no references, no faces, and go back to WOs with galleries. Proof of legality without proof of quality does not convert.
3. The fraud news (Dec 2025) has faded; couples in happy planning mode read a money-anxiety page, feel the brand is defensive, and the heavy 45-minute ask leaves few leads.

## Contradictions with the research
- #3 lists "references" as a question couples ask; the plan drops references entirely instead of offering "referensi pasangan sebelumnya, atas permintaan" (no invention needed).
- Customers "decide by recommendations": ignored.
- #3 says storyline beats follow the couple's questions in their order; the storyline leads with money flow (S2), and "coordinate only or also decorate" is never answered.
- #4 says show prices so visitors can compare; price is section 6.
- #5 suggests a schedule to share with family; not in the storyline.
- Research: couples plan 6-12 months out; the rundown starts at H-180.
- Research has 0 customer-voice sources; the strategy treats journalists' advice lists as "their wording" without saying so.

## Verdict
change. Demanded:
1. Make the roof survive the client's answer: confirm the payment model first, or rewrite to a true claim ("Bayar vendor langsung ke vendor. Fee kami bertahap, sesuai pekerjaan.") and keep a fallback roof.
2. Add a competence beat: named coordinator, a real anonymised rundown, references on request, portfolio slot marked [CLIENT].
3. Answer "jadi saya urus vendor sendiri?" in section 2: what the WO does around vendor payments.
4. Move price to section 3 or merge it with Alur uang; add a CTA after it and a sticky WhatsApp button.
5. Offer "Cek tanggal" as the first ask; the consultation follows.
6. Limit the rundown motif to the sections that are truly timed, or reorder the storyline chronologically; do not force H-labels on checks and price.
7. Fix H-180 vs 12 months and DP vs fee-after-milestone; add a line for parents; widen the inventory beyond one ceremony tradition.
