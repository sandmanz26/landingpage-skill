# Research: wedding organizer, Jakarta (2026-11-01)
Sources read: 3 opened (Jawa Pos, Popbela, Metro TV) + 2 search result sets · Competitors: price points of 6 WOs from search snippets (not opened) · Customer-voice sources: 0 opened (hipwee.com blocked by Cloudflare; reviews not reached) · Web access: partial

## What changes the design
| # | Finding | Type | Evidence | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Trust is the live issue in this category: a Jakarta WO took money from about 230 couples (losses Rp 16-20 billion) in 2024-2025, luring them with promo all-in packages, free venues and bonuses | Fact | Jawa Pos, 10 Dec 2025 (S1) | The page must answer "will my money be safe?" before it asks for anything. No promo-bonus language anywhere |
| 2 | The advice couples now read: check the business identity (office address, official accounts, legality), contact vendors directly, pay in stages tied to progress, keep contracts and receipts, ask where the money goes | Fact | Metro TV, tips after the WO Marwah case (S2) | Make these checks easy on the page itself: address, legal identity, vendor list, payment schedule, sample contract. They are the proof the business can show without testimonials |
| 3 | Couples are advised to ask: how many weddings you run a month; how you charge and what costs extra (crew meals, parking, lodging); whether you only coordinate or also decorate; your vendor list; whether you know my venue; whether you can keep my budget; how you handle problems and your backup plan; references | Fact | Popbela, "9 pertanyaan" (S3) | These are the customer's questions in their order: the storyline beats follow them, and the FAQ uses their wording |
| 4 | Prices run from about Rp 9 juta (day-of coordination) through Rp 30-40 juta (full packages) to Rp 200-300 juta (all-in at hotels); all-in packages bundle venue, catering, decor, documentation | Inference | Search snippets naming 5 WOs and Kompas TV (S4), not opened; the fraud packages were Rp 73-165 juta for 500 pax (S1) | Show prices per service, separate from vendor costs, so the visitor can compare. Say plainly what is and is not included |
| 5 | Parents often co-decide and co-pay | Assumption | Common in Indonesian weddings; not verified here. Check with the client's chat history | Copy speaks to the couple but keeps a line for "orang tua" (e.g. a schedule they can share with family) |
| 6 | Awareness level: comparing solutions (they know WOs exist and are choosing one) | Inference | All three sources are "how to choose" articles (S1-S3) | Lead with the difference that matters for choosing, not with "why use a WO" |

## Business
A wedding organizer earns from service fees. The action that pays is the first consultation, which leads to a contract. Proof available: none (no client). Missing proof is a finding: testimonials, number of weddings and portfolio must come from the real client.

## Competitors
| Competitor | Promise | Offer / price | Page structure | Proof used | Visual conventions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typical Jakarta WO (from search snippets and the fraud case) | "your dream wedding" | packages from Rp 9 juta to Rp 300 juta, promo discounts and bonuses | hero photo of a couple, packages, gallery, testimonials, WhatsApp | testimonials, gallery, Instagram follower counts | gold or blush, script fonts, floral ornaments, couple photo hero |
Category clichés: couple photo hero, script typeface, gold and blush, floral frames, "promo" bonuses. → Break the photo hero (no real photos exist) and the promo language (finding 1). Keep warmth, drop the script-and-gold look.

## Customers
Situation: a couple 6-12 months before the date, comparing WOs after reading about WO fraud. Want: a day that runs without them having to manage it, and money that does not disappear. Fears: losing deposits, vendors not showing up, hidden costs, family conflict on the day. Decide by: trust, clarity of costs, recommendations.
Words they use (from the articles, not reviews): "WO bodong", "DP", "hari H", "technical meeting", "rundown", "vendor", "biaya tambahan", "bertahap".

Available: nothing real. Missing: everything about this client. The page can show verifiable policies (payment schedule, vendor contacts, sample contract) once the client confirms them.
