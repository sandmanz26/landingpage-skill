# Strategy: Jalinan Wedding Organizer (v2, after the roast)

## Business canvas
- Action that pays: a free 45-minute consultation booked on WhatsApp (leads to a contract)
- Success measure: consultations booked per week
- Offer [SAMPLE, client to confirm]: three services priced as a fee for the WO's work only, separate from vendor costs: Hari H (coordination on the day), Pendampingan (6 months of planning + the day), Penuh (12 months + vendor selection + the day). Fee paid in three stages tied to milestones
- Position: against WOs that sell all-in promo packages and hold the couple's money (#1). The difference: **the couple pays vendors directly under their own contracts; the WO only takes its fee, in stages**
- Proof the business can show: verifiable policies (address, legal identity, vendor contacts, sample contract) (#2). Missing: testimonials, wedding count, portfolio photos
- Constraints: no invented proof; no promo-bonus language (#1)

## Customer profile
A couple in Jakarta, 6-12 months before the date, often deciding with their parents (#5, assumption). They have read about WO fraud (#1) and are comparing WOs (#6). Want the day to run without them managing it, and their money safe. Afraid of: losing the DP, vendors not showing up, hidden costs (crew meals, parking, lodging), chaos on the day (#3). Decide by trust and clarity of cost. Words: "DP", "hari H", "rundown", "technical meeting", "vendor", "biaya tambahan", "bertahap".
Awareness level: comparing solutions (#6)

## Message house
Roof: "Hari H kami yang pegang. Uangnya tetap di tangan Anda."
Pillars:
1. Uangnya jelas: vendors are paid by you directly; our fee in 3 stages after each milestone; full cost breakdown before any DP (#2)
2. Bisa dicek sebelum bayar: office address, NIB, vendor list with phone numbers, sample contract to read first (#2)
3. Hari H terpegang: one wedding per day, named coordinator from the first meeting, rundown from H-90, technical meeting, rehearsal, backup plan (#3)
Objections: hidden costs → stated in each price; "only a coordinator?" → say exactly what we do and don't; unfamiliar venue → site visit included; budget → budget plan; problems → backup plan examples (#3)

## Subject inventory and idea
Inventory: rundown (susunan acara) with clock times; H-minus countdown (H-180, H-90, H-7); technical meeting; gladi resik; HT radios of the crew; name tags of the crew; vendor list with phone numbers; the contract; payment receipts ("kuitansi"); the guest book; janur / melati in Javanese weddings; akad then resepsi order.
> On this page, **the rundown — the WO's own timed working document — becomes the page's structure**: each section is a timed line, and the payment stages are written into the schedule beside the work they pay for.
Competitor test: every WO has a rundown, but putting the money into the schedule carries the roof ("money stays with you, released only when work is done"); a promo all-in WO cannot write that sentence.

## Open questions for the client
Real prices and what they include; NIB and office address; vendor list; max weddings per day; crew size per guest count; real wedding count and reviews; photos of real weddings.
