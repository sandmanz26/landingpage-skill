## Top problems and what was done
1. Crew fixed per package in the price cards, but by guest count in the day section; "biaya tambahan: tidak ada" next to it: a 600-guest couple could not tell the price (conversion, customer). Fixed: crew removed from the cards, prices say "mulai", a table of guests → crew → added fee under the cards [SAMPLE], "no other costs outside this table".
2. The 30/40/30 stages sat in the cards' three-column grid and read as one number per package (art director). Fixed: a title "Fee dibayar dalam 3 tahap, sama untuk semua paket" and one bar split 3:4:3.
3. On phones the header, the clock bar and the WhatsApp bar stacked to about 23% of the screen in the day section (motion). Fixed: the WhatsApp bar steps aside while the day section is on screen; scroll padding top and bottom.
4. "Kalian" alone on a line on phones (customer, art director). Fixed with a non-breaking space.
5. 17 font sizes (art, motion). Reduced to 13 by snapping small sizes to a scale; WARN kept: the clock, h1, h2, prices and the close line are deliberate display sizes.

## Not fixed, with the reason
- Proof of quality (story, customer: "the page proves you are real, not that you are good"; asked for past weddings, a quote, a count). Not added: proof is never invented (SKILL.md). This is the first item in "Supaya lebih bagus" for the client.
