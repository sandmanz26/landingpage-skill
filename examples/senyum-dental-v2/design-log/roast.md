# Roast: Senyum Dental (2026-10-30)
Note: in this demonstration the roast was written in a separate step that read only the brief, research.md and strategy.md, not by an independent subagent.

## Points
| # | Voice | Point | Evidence | Severity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sceptical customer | Nothing tells me I need this now. I have postponed for two years and nothing bad happened. The page starts by selling convenience to someone who has not decided to go. | research #1 (57% feel no need) | high |
| 2 | Sceptical customer | Rp 350.000 when the puskesmas is Rp 50–200k and a dental hospital is on the same road. The page never says why the difference is worth it. | research #4, #5 | high |
| 3 | Sceptical customer | "Bisa BPJS?" is the first thing I would ask on WhatsApp. If the page is silent, I message to ask and maybe never book. | research #7 | medium |
| 4 | Competitor's marketer | Speed is easy to copy: mall clinics are open late too. "30 minutes" alone is not a moat. Your real difference is the scheduled slot plus everything explained first. | research #5, #6 | medium |
| 5 | The owner | There is no proof on the page at all: no rating, no dentist names, no photos. This is the weakest link for a health purchase, and the plan just accepts it. | research, Proof | high |
| 6 | The owner | The booking button does not say how fast we reply. Customers in reviews praise "reserved at night, came the next day". | research #6 | medium |
| 7 | Senior art director | The clock carries only the Time pillar. Clarity and Calm, two of the three pillars, have no visual device; the page will read as "fast", not as "you will be looked after". | message house | medium |
| 8 | Senior art director | A whole section for fear risks making a minority worry the theme of the page for everyone. | research #2 | low |

## Pre-mortem
1. Visitors who feel no need never get past the hero: the page answered "how" before "why".
2. People compare the price on Google listings first and never open the page; the page that does open must justify the price immediately.
3. WhatsApp replies come slowly at lunch time, so the bookings the page earns are lost (operational, outside the page; flag it to the client).

## Contradictions with the research
- The strategy treats fear and time as the main barriers; the research says the main barrier is "not needed" (#1). The storyline has no beat for it.
- The strategy says compete on clarity, but the price section only states the price; it does not show what the price buys (#4).

## Verdict
**Change.** Keep the direction family (split stage with the clock), change the storyline and what the clock carries.

## Response
| # | Decision | What changed, or why not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Accepted | New beat 2 "Perlu sekarang?": three signs that it is time (bleeding gums, persistent bad breath, visible tartar) and "every 6 months" as the dentist's rule; text to be confirmed by the clinic's dentist. |
| 2 | Accepted | The price section shows what Rp 350.000 buys against the alternatives, without naming them: a fixed time instead of a queue, an explanation before treatment, one price. No disparaging comparison. |
| 3 | Accepted | One line under the price answering BPJS; marked [SAMPLE] until the clinic confirms its policy. |
| 4 | Accepted | Roof rewritten to lead with "jadwal pasti, dijelaskan dulu"; 30 minutes becomes the proof, not the promise. |
| 5 | Accepted as a launch blocker | Proof cannot be invented. Dentist names and credentials, the Google rating and 6 photos are listed as required before launch. The page reserves a section for them; until then it is marked [SAMPLE] and the report says the page is not ready to publish. |
| 6 | Accepted | Under the button: "Dibalas dalam 10 menit pada jam buka" [SAMPLE: client to confirm the real reply time]. |
| 7 | Accepted, changes the direction | The clock becomes the visit's script: at each minute it shows the time **and** what the dentist explains and what you may feel. It now carries Time, Clarity and Calm. |
| 8 | Accepted in part | No separate fear section. Fear is answered inside the clock's steps ("you may feel vibration; raise your hand to pause"), where it arises. |
