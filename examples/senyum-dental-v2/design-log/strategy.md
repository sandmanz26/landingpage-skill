# Strategy: Senyum Dental (v2, after the roast)

## Business canvas
- Action that pays: a WhatsApp booking for check-up + cleaning.
- Success measure: bookings per week from the page; click-to-message rate.
- Offer: check-up + cleaning, 30 minutes, Rp 350.000, a scheduled slot; risk-reducer: nothing is done without explaining it first, no extra cost without consent.
- Position: upper-middle price (research #4). Against the cheaper hospital on the same road (#5) and the puskesmas, compete on a fixed time near the office and clarity, not on price.
- Proof available: price, duration, hours, address, 3 dentists. Missing: reviews, dentists' names, photos.
- Constraints: health advertising must not promise outcomes or "painless" treatment (#3).

## Customer profile
Office workers near Jl. Riau, 25–45, reading on a phone at lunch. They know they should go and keep postponing. Awareness level: aware of the problem, postponing (research #1).
They fear losing time, being surprised by the bill, and some fear pain (#2, #3). They decide on time, clarity of price, and trust.

## Message house
Roof: **Jadwal pasti di jam istirahat, semuanya dijelaskan dulu: 30 menit, Rp 350.000.** (roast #4)
Pillars:
1. Time: a scheduled 30-minute slot, back at work by 12.30 (brief).
2. Clarity: one price, known before; every step explained before it is done (brief, #4).
3. Calm: honest about what you will feel; you can stop at any time (#3).
Objections: "Sakit?" → pillar 3. "Mahal?" → what the price buys (#4). "Lama?" → pillar 1. "BPJS?" → answered (client to confirm, #7).

## Subject inventory and idea
Inventory as in v1 (name "Senyum", 30 minutes, Rp 350.000, Jl. Riau 12, 09.00–20.00, 3 dentists, WhatsApp, the lunch break, the appointment slot).
Idea: On this page, **the 30-minute lunch-break visit** becomes **a clock that advances 12.00 → 12.30 and, at each minute, says what is being done, what is explained and what you may feel** (roast #7). Competitor test: passes (no competitor promises this slot at this address and price).
