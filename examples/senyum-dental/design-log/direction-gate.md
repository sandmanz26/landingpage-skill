# Direction gate (what the user would see in guided mode)

**A. The split stage**: recommended
On this page, **the 30-minute lunch-break visit** becomes **the fixed half of the screen: a clock face that moves from 12.00 to 12.30 as the visitor scrolls through what happens in each step**.
Composition: sticky clock on one side, a calm reading column on the other; one section (price) breaks the split and runs full width.
Type and colour: a soft serif for the name and the times, a calm sans for reading; paper ground with the brand green #0F5C4D as the one ink accent.
Why it fits: the brief's strongest fact is "30 minutes"; the fixed clock keeps that promise in view the whole time, which is exactly what an anxious, busy visitor needs. It needs no photographs, which we do not have.
Competitor test: another clinic cannot claim this clock (30 minutes, fixed Rp 350.000, Jl. Riau 12, open till 20.00). Passes.

**B. Colour chapters**
On this page, **each five minutes of the visit** becomes **its own full-screen colour chapter**.
Why not first: loud flat fields work against a calm page for people who are afraid of the dentist; the brand gives one green, not a set of chapter colours.

**C. The place carries it**
On this page, **the practice room** becomes **one full-bleed photograph**.
Why not: it depends entirely on a photograph of the place, and none exists yet. Worth redealing once the clinic's six photos arrive.

Recommendation: A.
