# Argument: Senyum Dental

## Subject inventory (only this clinic, from the brief)
1. The name "Senyum" (smile): the outcome, not the procedure.
2. 30 minutes for check-up + cleaning: it fits inside a lunch break.
3. Rp 350.000, one price, known before the visit.
4. Jl. Riau 12, Bandung: a street of offices and shops, reachable at lunch.
5. Open Monday–Saturday 09.00–20.00: before work, at lunch, after work.
6. Three dentists: someone is always on duty in opening hours.
7. Booking through WhatsApp: the channel the audience already has open.
8. The audience's fear: pain, surprise costs, losing time.
9. The audience's moment: on a phone, during the break, deciding quickly.
10. The printed thing of this world: the appointment card / time slot.

Competitor test on the lead fact: "another clinic in Bandung, 30 minutes, Rp 350.000, open till 20.00 at Jl. Riau 12" does not hold for a competitor; the half-hour-in-a-lunch-break claim plus the fixed price is this clinic's.
