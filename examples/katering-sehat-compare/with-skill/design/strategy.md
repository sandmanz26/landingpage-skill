# Strategy: Dapur Seimbang (v1)

## Business canvas
- **Action that pays:** a WhatsApp message to 0812-3456-7890 that starts a subscription (min. 5 days).
- **Success measure:** WhatsApp chats per week from the page, and the share that become a paid 5-day start.
- **Offer:** lunch delivered to the office, Mon–Fri, Rp 45.000/porsi, min. 5 days = Rp 225.000. Every box: karbo (nasi merah or shirataki) + lauk (ayam / ikan / tahu tempe) + sayur + buah; menu changes daily. Risk-reducer: the minimum is only one work week (no monthly lock-in) (#1).
- **Position:** against the lunch you buy near the office every day (#7) and against diet caterers (#2): a normal, balanced lunch, not a diet programme, at a price between the two (#1).
- **Proof the business can show:** the price, the box structure, the days and area, the real number (#6). Missing: photos, reviews, delivery window, nutrition (#6).
- **Constraints:** no photos, no logo, no brand colours; a small kitchen answering WhatsApp by hand; no health claims beyond what the menu structure shows (no calories, no weight loss).

## Customer profile
Office worker in Jakarta Selatan, 25–40, decides lunch every day between meetings, wants to eat better but has no time to cook or pack bekal; reads on a phone. Wants: "makan siang sehat datang sendiri, nggak usah mikir tiap hari." Decides by price per day, taste and fullness, whether it reaches their office, and how easy it is to start. Afraid of: bland "diet" food, the same menu every day, getting locked into a long package, late delivery. Words: makan siang, langganan, nasi merah, shirataki, porsi, antar ke kantor, menu ganti tiap hari, nggak sempat masak.
Awareness level: **comparing solutions** (research #2, #7; Inference) — they know healthy catering exists and compare it with GoFood/warteg and diet caterers.

## Message house
Roof: **"Makan siang seimbang, diantar ke kantor Anda di Jakarta Selatan, Senin–Jumat — menunya ganti tiap hari."** (v2 after roast #1)
Pillars:
1. **Seimbang di setiap kotak** — always four parts: karbo (nasi merah/shirataki), lauk, sayur, buah. Proof: the fixed structure, shown as the box itself (owner's facts).
2. **Menu ganti tiap hari** — a different filling Mon–Fri. Proof: the week shown day by day (dish names `[SAMPLE]` until the owner sends the real rotation) (#3).
3. **Harga jelas, mulai dari satu minggu** — Rp 45.000/porsi, Rp 225.000 for 5 days, ordered in one WhatsApp chat (#1, #5).
Objections (foundation): "sehat = hambar / nggak kenyang" → show the full plate with real lauk (ayam, ikan, tahu tempe), not salad (#2, #4); "bosan" → the week view (#3); "kemahalan" → per-day price beside the weekly total, framed as one week, not a programme (#1); "kantor saya kejangkau?" → Jakarta Selatan stated plainly, exact area confirmed on WhatsApp (#4); "ribet pesannya" → three steps and a pre-filled message (#5).

## Subject inventory and idea
Only this client: (1) the name *Seimbang* (balanced); (2) the fixed four-part rule karbo–lauk–sayur–buah; (3) the choice nasi merah **or** shirataki; (4) lauk trio ayam / ikan / tahu tempe; (5) the minimum of 5 days = exactly one work week Mon–Fri; (6) Rp 45.000; (7) delivered to offices in Jakarta Selatan; (8) ordering by WhatsApp to one real number; (9) daily rotation; (10) an owner who writes "kak" — a small kitchen, not a platform.

> On this page, **the four-part Dapur Seimbang lunch box (karbo, lauk, sayur, buah)** becomes **the page's module: a box whose four compartments stay fixed while the filling changes with each weekday the visitor taps**.

Carries the roof: balance (fixed compartments) + no thinking about the menu (it changes for you). Competitor test: "Yellow Fit's diet box becomes…" — Yellow Fit sells calorie-counted diet meals; their device would be calories/macros, not four fixed parts + a 5-day minimum week. The sentence fits Dapur Seimbang's name and rule; partly category-level (any bento caterer has compartments), so the distinctiveness must come from the week (Mon–Fri = the minimum order) and the name's balance, carried into price and order sections too.
