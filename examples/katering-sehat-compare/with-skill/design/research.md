## Business
Offer: lunch box delivered to offices in Jakarta Selatan, Mon–Fri, menu changes daily (nasi merah or shirataki + ayam/ikan/tahu tempe + sayur + buah). Price Rp 45.000/porsi, minimum 5 days (Rp 225.000). Action that pays: a WhatsApp message to 0812-3456-7890 starting a subscription. Proof available: price, menu structure, area, days, number. Constraints: no photos, no brand, no reviews; small business answering WhatsApp by hand (so the WhatsApp message should arrive pre-filled with what the owner needs to know: name, office address, start date, nasi merah/shirataki).

## Customers
Situation: office worker in Jakarta Selatan, 25–40, at a desk or on the phone, deciding lunch daily; wants to eat better but has no time to cook or prepare bekal. · Job to be done: "Makan siang yang sehat datang sendiri ke kantor, tanpa mikir tiap hari." · Awareness level: **solution aware / comparing** (Inference: they know katering sehat exists; the owner says buyers already want healthy food) · How they decide: price per day, whether it tastes good, whether it actually arrives at their office on time, whether ordering is easy.

## Hypotheses to test after launch
- H1: Showing the weekly total (Rp 225.000) next to the per-portion price raises WhatsApp clicks vs. per-portion alone. Metric: WhatsApp click rate.
- H2: The week-menu flipper raises scroll depth to the order section. Metric: % reaching #pesan.
